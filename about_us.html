About Us

Thusi's Bakery is a small local business located in Noordwyk, Midrand. The bakery provides fresh and affordable baked goods to the community.

The bakery sells bread, cakes, muffins, and vetkoek (magwinya). All products are made daily using fresh ingredients to ensure good quality and taste.

Thusi's Bakery also offers online ordering and delivery services. These services make it easy for customers to buy products from home. The bakery also supports small events such as birthdays and family gatherings.

The aim of the bakery is to provide excellent service, satisfy customers, and grow the business in the future.
